Frequently Asked Questions

What makes EcoMart products sustainable?+

All products at EcoMart undergo a rigorous sustainability assessment. We evaluate each product based on materials used, manufacturing processes, packaging, transportation carbon footprint, and end-of-life recyclability. We partner only with certified eco-friendly suppliers and ensure transparency in our sourcing and production methods.

How do you ship your products sustainably?+

We use carbon-neutral shipping partners and offset 100% of our shipping emissions. Our packaging is 100% recycled and recyclable, and we minimize packaging where possible. For local deliveries, we use electric vehicles and bike couriers in urban areas.

What is your return policy?+

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused items in original packaging. For sustainability reasons, we encourage customers to donate unwanted items to local charities instead of returning them. If a return is necessary, we provide a prepaid return label and donate returned items to partner organizations.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk ordering?+

Yes! We offer wholesale pricing for businesses and organizations. Bulk discounts are available for orders over 50 items. Please contact our wholesale team at wholesale@ecomart.com for pricing and partnership opportunities.

How can I track my order?+

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "My Orders" section. For real-time updates, you can use our live tracking map available in shipping notifications.

Are your products certified organic or fair trade?+

Many of our products carry certifications such as USDA Organic, Fair Trade, B Corp, and others. Each product page clearly displays applicable certifications. For products without third-party certification, we provide detailed information about our sustainability practices and supplier relationships.
